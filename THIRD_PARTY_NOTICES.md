## Specimen photographs

The JPEG files are in `client/public/specimens/`. Some are reduced-resolution
Commons previews. The viewer resizes and crops thumbnails for display. It does
not apply a color filter. Earlier versions applied CSS saturation and contrast
adjustments; those display filters have been removed. Source URLs identify the
original composition and its terms. Preserve attribution and applicable
share-alike terms when redistributing or adapting these photographs.

A mapped example locality is separate from the pictured specimen's origin.

## Fonts and dependencies

The page requests Instrument Sans, Instrument Serif, and IBM Plex Mono through
Google Fonts, with local fallbacks. No font binaries are bundled. Dependencies
retain their own licenses; package-manager metadata records their versions.
